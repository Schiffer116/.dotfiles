# Glossary

**Palette**: the one set of colours the whole desktop uses: Catppuccin Mocha, with
Mocha's darkest shade (crust) as the main background instead of its usual base.

**Role**: a palette colour named for its job rather than its hue, so configs say
what a colour is for:

- **bg**: the main background (crust).
- **surface**: panels, buttons and anything raised above **bg**.
- **text**: normal foreground text.
- **accent**: focus and highlight, such as the active border or selected item (lavender).
- **ok**, **warn**, **urgent**: good, needs attention, and error or alarm (green,
  yellow, red).

**Official theme**: a theme published by Catppuccin for one app (nvim's plugin,
herdr's built-in theme, mako's). An app with one uses it unchanged and keeps
Mocha's usual base background, rather than taking colours from the **Palette**.
